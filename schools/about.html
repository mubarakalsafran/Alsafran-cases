An independent guide for parents

What is in this directory, where the numbers come from, and how to correct them.

What this is

Kuwait Schools Guide is an independent directory of schools in Kuwait — American, British, IB, Indian CBSE, Arabic-curriculum and the national public system, plus nurseries and kindergartens. Every listing carries the curriculum, the annual tuition for each grade band, the years offered, the district with a map pin, the school's Instagram account and reviews written by signed-in parents.

It is not affiliated with the Ministry of Education or with any school.

Data & accuracy

Read this before you rely on a fee figure. The catalogue that ships with this site is seed data. School names, districts and curricula are real. The fee figures, some Instagram handles and all phone numbers are indicative placeholders set at realistic levels for each curriculum tier — they have not been confirmed with the schools, and every listing is flagged pending confirmation until an administrator verifies it.

Where we do not know a school's official Instagram handle, the Instagram icon opens an Instagram search for the school's name rather than guessing at an account that might belong to someone else.

Tuition also rarely tells the whole story: registration, books, uniform, transport and exam entry are usually billed on top. Always confirm the current year's figures directly with the school.

Corrections are welcome. A school can claim its listing and an administrator updates it from the dashboard; the change is stored against that school and shown to every visitor.

How reviews work

Only signed-in accounts can post a review, one review per school per account. Every submission is held unpublished until a moderator approves it, which is what keeps competitor spam and fake five-star runs out of the ratings. A school's star rating is the mean of its published reviews; the homepage only ranks schools that have at least two, so a single glowing review cannot put a school at the top.

Reviews are parents' own opinions. They are not verified claims of fact, and they are not the directory's view of a school.

This build

This is a complete, dependency-free front end. Accounts, reviews, favourites and the admin dashboard's edits are stored in your own browser, so the whole platform is demonstrable from a static host — but that also means data does not travel between devices, and the prototype auth in this build is not production security. See the repository README for what a real deployment needs to replace.
