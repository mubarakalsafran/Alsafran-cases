An independent guide for parents

What is in this directory, where the numbers come from, and how to correct them.

What this is

Kuwait Schools Guide is an independent directory of private schools in Kuwait — American, British, IB and Indian CBSE, plus nurseries and kindergartens. Every listing carries the curriculum, the annual tuition for each grade band, the years offered, the district with a map pin, the school's Instagram account and reviews written by signed-in parents.

It is not affiliated with the Ministry of Education or with any school.

We cover private schools only. Government schools are not listed: places there are assigned by residential catchment rather than chosen, and tuition is free for Kuwaiti citizens, so there is nothing to compare.

Data & accuracy

Fees in this directory come at three levels of confidence, and every school carries a badge saying which. Fees from the school’s website means we took the figures from the school’s own official site. Published fee data means they come from a published fee directory we cross-checked. Estimate — not confirmed means the school publishes nothing and we found no source, so the number is a guess and should not be relied on.

We checked that directory against the schools themselves: the American School of Kuwait and Al-Bayan Bilingual School match exactly between their own websites and the directory, which is why we trust it — but it is still not the school, so it gets a different badge.

Some schools publish no fees at all — The British School of Kuwait is one — and for those we say so plainly and give you the route to ask them, instead of inventing a number.

Private school fees in Kuwait are set and approved by the Ministry of Education and can change year to year, so every figure is shown with the academic year it belongs to.

Where we do not know a school's official Instagram handle, the Instagram icon opens an Instagram search for the school's name rather than guessing at an account that might belong to someone else.

Tuition also rarely tells the whole story: registration, books, uniform, transport and exam entry are usually billed on top. Always confirm the current year's figures directly with the school.

Corrections are welcome. A school can claim its listing and an administrator updates it from the dashboard; the change is stored against that school and shown to every visitor.

How reviews work

We have not written a single review. Every school in this directory starts with an empty comment section, and the stars you see are only ever what parents actually wrote. Nothing was pre-written to make the directory look busy.

Only signed-in accounts can post a review, one review per school per account. Every submission is held unpublished until a moderator approves it, which is what keeps competitor spam and fake five-star runs out of the ratings. A school's star rating is the mean of its published reviews; the homepage only ranks schools that have at least two, so a single glowing review cannot put a school at the top.

Reviews are parents' own opinions. They are not verified claims of fact, and they are not the directory's view of a school.

This build

This is a complete, dependency-free front end. Accounts, reviews, favourites and the admin dashboard's edits are stored in your own browser, so the whole platform is demonstrable from a static host — but that also means data does not travel between devices, and the prototype auth in this build is not production security.
