# 11. Recommendation, Budget & First 10 Actions

## 11.1 The strongest brand direction — one paragraph

> **ALSAFRAN is a Kuwait-born carry label built on drop culture and Arabic typography.**
> Keep the family name — it makes you a house rather than a shop, and "safran" means saffron
> in half of Europe and Asia, which hands you a ready-made story about the most precious cargo
> that ever passed through the Gulf. Position at **affordable-premium**: a 5.900 KD clear case
> as the door, **8.900 KD as the hero price**, 13.500 KD leather and lined-silicone as the
> halo, and 16.900 KD numbered drops as the story. Compete on three things a catalogue reseller
> structurally cannot copy — **capped, numbered drops you actually honour; Arabic typography
> drawn properly; and football/youth-culture timing** — then add **72-hour in-house
> personalisation** by month four, which turns the whole thing into a gifting business at 76%
> margin. Make every case MagSafe-native so the case becomes the entry point to a colour-matched
> Mag system, and the brand grows by average order value rather than by chasing new customers.
> Look like an international label from day one — off-white not white, one gold accent, real
> photography on real people, a rigid box worth filming — and let "from Kuwait" be a fact in the
> story rather than the whole positioning.

**The one-line version:** *the Gulf's first drop-culture accessories label — numbered, never
restocked, with your name set properly in Arabic in three days.*

### Why this direction over the alternatives
| Alternative | Why not |
|---|---|
| Cheap, high-volume case shop (3–6 KD) | You'd be competing with every reseller in Kuwait on price, with no route to a lifestyle brand. It forfeits the entire premise of the brief. |
| Full luxury (20–35 KD leather only) | Cuts out students and football kids — most of your target audience — and needs 2–3× the launch capital and brand equity you don't have yet. Correct for the *Atelier tier*, wrong for the *brand*. |
| Print-on-demand / dropship | Zero moat, 2–4 week delivery, no packaging control, no drops. Anyone can do it tomorrow, which is why everyone has. |
| Generic "Kuwait pride" designs | Works for one National Day, then dies. Heritage has to be *design DNA* (Sadu redrawn, Qahwa tonal type), not a flag on a case. |

### What each scenario actually buys you

**Minimum (~2,800 KD)** — 5 designs, 4 models, ~500 units, decent packaging, DIY photography,
a Shopify store and almost no ad budget. It works, and plenty of brands have started here, but
everything depends on your content being good because you can't buy reach. Personalisation
waits until revenue funds the printer.

**Recommended (~5,160 KD)** ⭐ — the Lean buy plan from doc 9: 8 designs, 760 units, proper
identity work, licensed Arabic type, a real shoot, a real box, 30 creators seeded and enough ad
budget to learn what converts. **This is the smallest amount of money that buys the brand
described in these documents rather than a smaller version of it.** Expected sell-through
revenue ≈ 6,850 KD, so you're capital-positive inside the first collection if it sells.

**Well-funded (~12,420 KD)** — the full 11-design collection, the UV printer from day one
(so personalisation and the whole gifting business launch immediately), professional
photography, and enough ad budget to reach Saudi and the UAE within six months.

### Cash flow reality — the thing that kills first-time brands
You pay the factory ~10 weeks before you take a single dinar. Sequence the spend:

| When | Out | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Weeks 1–2 | ~1,130 KD | Licence, trademark, identity, Arabic type licence |
| Week 4 | ~180 KD | Samples |
| Week 6 | ~480 KD deposit | 30% of the production order |
| Week 8 | ~450 KD | Packaging (paper items locally, boxes with the case shipment) |
| Week 9 | ~1,120 KD | 70% production balance + freight + duty, on approved inspection photos |
| Weeks 9–11 | ~700 KD | Photography + website |
| Weeks 11–13 | ~550 KD | Seeding + launch ads |
| **Week 13** | **first revenue** | |

**Hold back at least 450 KD of untouched contingency past launch day.** Something will go wrong
— a bad batch, a wrong cutout, a courier failure — and the ability to fix it instantly without
arguing with a customer is worth more than the extra ad spend.

**Break-even:** roughly **36 orders/month** covers fixed overheads (~170 KD/month). Recovering
the full 5,160 KD investment plus six months of overheads takes roughly **1,000 cases sold** at
the recommended mix (~6.300 KD gross profit per case) — inside your first two drops if drop 01
sells through.

## 11.3 What to launch with — exactly

**Launch with 8 designs, 4 iPhone models, 760 units, one collection, one story.** Nothing more.

| Launch with | Don't launch with |
|---|---|
| **8 designs:** Crystal, Yalla (Core) · Ink, Bone, Safran, Qahwa (Signature) · Lulu (Atelier) · 965 (Limited, 100 numbered) | 11+ designs. Save Sadu, Pitch and Dhow for "part two", 6 weeks later — a second sell-out moment at no extra cash today |
| **4 models:** iPhone 17 Pro Max, 17 Pro, 16 Pro Max, 16 Pro | Android, older iPhones, or "all models". Add them once you own a printer and can produce on demand |
| **3 price points:** 5.900 · 8.900 · 13.500, plus 16.900 numbered | A 4.900 KD entry case. It drags the whole brand down and it's a fight you can't win |
| **One collection story:** Gold Route | Random designs with no thread between them |
| **Real packaging** from order one | "We'll upgrade the box later." The box is the marketing |
| **Own photography**, shot vertical | A single supplier catalogue image, ever |
| **KNET + Apple Pay + Tabby + WhatsApp** | Card-only checkout. It silently kills a large share of Kuwaiti conversions |
| **Personalisation as a waitlist** — "coming in 8 weeks, join the list" | Promising 3-day custom orders before you can deliver them |
| **A Drop Pass list** built from week 6 | Launching to a cold audience on day one |

**The three things that matter most on launch day, in order:** (1) the Drop Pass list is 300+
numbers, (2) the box is genuinely worth filming, (3) the first 30 orders ship within 24 hours
with a handwritten name on the card. Everything else can be fixed in week two.

## 11.4 The first 10 actions

Do these in order. Actions 1–5 cost almost nothing and can all be done in the next 10 days.

### 1 · Lock the brand assets — *today, ~1 hour, ~15 KD*
Buy `alsafran.com` (then `.co` as backup). Register `@alsafran.co` on **Instagram, TikTok,
Snapchat, X and YouTube** in one sitting, plus a WhatsApp Business number that is not your
personal line. Same handle everywhere, monogram as the profile picture, 2FA on all of them.
This is the cheapest irreversible decision you'll make — do it before you tell anyone the name.

### 2 · File the trademark and the licence — *this week, ~480 KD*
ALSAFRAN in **class 9** (phone cases and covers) and **class 18** (bags and carry) through a
Kuwaiti trademark agent, and the e-commerce licence via **Sahel / MOCI**. Filing early is
cheap; filing after someone has seen your launch and registered it first is a catastrophe.

### 3 · Commission the wordmark — *start this week, 10–14 days, ~400 KD*
Brief a type-literate freelance designer with [`01-brand-identity.md`](01-brand-identity.md).
Deliverables: `ALSAFRAN` wordmark in **Latin and Arabic as one locked-up system**, the A/S
monogram, the Saffron Thread mark, and files in SVG/PDF/PNG. Nothing else gets designed until
this exists.

### 4 · Email 15 factories — *today, ~2 hours, free*
Use the exact email in [`08-launch-strategy.md`](08-launch-strategy.md#stage-2--finding-manufacturers--weeks-24-free).
Alibaba + 1688, Shenzhen/Guangzhou/Dongguan, own-factory only. Send 15 tonight; you'll have
5 real conversations by the end of the week. **This is the longest lead time in the whole plan
— every day you delay it delays launch day by a day.**

### 5 · Set up Instagram and TikTok and start posting — *this week, free*
Don't wait for product. Post the samples, the drop tests, the type explorations, the box
mock-ups. Nine planned grid posts, five highlights, the bio from doc 1. **Seven weeks of
posting history before launch is worth more than any launch-day ad budget**, and it costs
nothing but consistency.

### 6 · Order samples from 3 factories — *week 3–4, ~180 KD*
Same brief to all three, express shipping, and make sure one Arabic design is printed in every
sample batch. How a factory renders Arabic tells you whether they can make your best-selling
family.

### 7 · Test them properly and film everything — *week 6, one evening, free*
Run the full test matrix from doc 8: 2 m drops onto concrete, 200 button presses, the denim
rub, the dashboard heat test, the MagSafe hang. **Film all of it.** This footage is your best
pre-launch content, your best "why we're different" proof, and the reason you'll never ship a
bad batch.

### 8 · Open the Drop Pass and start collecting numbers — *week 6, free*
A one-field landing page (name + WhatsApp number) and a link in both bios: *"200 of each. Drop
Pass members get in 30 minutes early."* **Target 300 numbers before launch day.** This list is
the single most valuable asset in the business and the only audience no algorithm can take
away from you.

### 9 · Place the production order — *week 6, ~480 KD deposit / 1,600 KD total*
The Lean buy plan: 8 designs, 760 units, 4 models. Trade Assurance, 30/70 payment split,
inspection photos before the balance, and the boxes and mailers shipping in the same
consignment as the cases.

### 10 · Book the shoot and build the site — *weeks 8–11, ~700 KD*
One photography day (studio morning, golden-hour location afternoon, 3 friends, vertical
first). In parallel: Shopify Basic, a custom theme built from the `/site` prototype in this
repo, **KNET + Apple Pay + Tabby live and tested with a real 1 KD order**. Then set the launch
date — **a Thursday at 8:00 PM** — and run the pre-launch sequence from doc 8.
