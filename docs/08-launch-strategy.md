# 8. Launch Strategy — from zero to the first 100 orders

**Total timeline: ~14 weeks from decision to launch day**, then 8 weeks to 100 orders.
Weeks 1–6 are cheap and mostly your own time. The money leaves in weeks 4–8.

```
W1─2   Brand creation           ░░
W2─4   Sourcing & suppliers       ░░░
W4─6   Samples in hand              ░░░
W6─7   Quality testing                ░░
W6─8   Packaging production          ░░░
W8─9   Photography                      ░░
W7─11  Website build                  ░░░░░
W6─14  Social build + pre-launch hype ░░░░░░░░
W10─12 Influencer seeding                 ░░░
W13    LAUNCH DAY                            █
W13─20 First 100 orders                      ░░░░░░
W20+   Retention → GCC
```

---

## Stage 1 · Brand creation — Weeks 1–2 (~350–700 KD)

- Lock the name, tagline and story (done — see [`01-brand-identity.md`](01-brand-identity.md)).
- Register the **company/e-commerce licence** in Kuwait (Sahel app / MOCI), open a business
  bank account, and file a **trademark application for ALSAFRAN in class 9** (phone cases) and
  **class 18** (bags/carry) — do this now, before you're visible enough to be copied.
- Commission the **wordmark + monogram in Arabic and Latin as one system** (250–600 KD to a
  type-literate freelancer on Behance/Dribbble/Instagram; brief them with doc 1).
- Buy the domain and lock every social handle the same day.
- Build the mini brand kit: logo files, colour tokens, two fonts, the photo LUT, four Canva/
  Figma story templates. From here on, nothing gets posted that isn't in the kit.

**Do not skip:** the trademark and the handles. Both get expensive to fix later.

---

## Stage 2 · Finding manufacturers — Weeks 2–4 (free)

**Where to look:** Alibaba and 1688 (with a sourcing agent) for **Shenzhen / Guangzhou /
Dongguan** case factories. Search terms that find real manufacturers rather than traders:
`magsafe phone case manufacturer OEM`, `liquid silicone case factory`, `custom phone case
private label`, `anti-yellow PC TPU case OEM`.

**Contact 12–15, expect 5 to reply properly, sample from 3.** Look for: 5+ years, verified
supplier, an *own factory* (ask for a video walkthrough of the line), OEM/private-label
experience, MOQ 300–500 per design, and a willingness to do custom packaging.

**The email that gets a serious reply** (send this, not "what's your price?"):
> Hi — I'm launching a design-led accessories brand in Kuwait. I need a manufacturing partner
> for MagSafe cases in three build levels: (1) twin-shot PC+TPU, (2) soft-touch PC with
> anodised aluminium buttons and a 12× N42 MagSafe array, (3) lined liquid silicone / leather
> over a PC frame. Models: iPhone 17 Pro Max, 17 Pro, 16 Pro Max, 16 Pro.
> Please send: FOB unit price at 300 / 500 / 1,000 pcs per design, MOQ, tooling cost if any,
> lead time, whether you can print/deboss custom artwork and produce custom rigid boxes, and
> your drop-test and yellowing test reports. I'll be ordering samples from three factories this
> month and placing a first PO within six weeks.

**Ask every factory for:** anti-yellowing spec on clear (Bayer/Covestro PC — get it in
writing), magnet grade and count, drop-test report, RoHS/REACH, and their *own* photos of the
soft-touch coating after abrasion testing.

**Also line up:** a local Kuwaiti printer for cards/stickers/spine labels, a rigid-box supplier
(often the case factory can do it), and a freight forwarder who handles Kuwait customs.

**Negotiation notes:** never accept the first price — 15–25% is normally available at 500 units.
Ask for tooling to be waived or amortised. Pay via Alibaba Trade Assurance for order one; never
wire 100% up front. Split payment 30% deposit / 70% on approved inspection photos.

## Stage 3 · Samples — Weeks 4–6 (~120–250 KD)

Order from **three** factories, the same brief, and compare like for like:
- 2 clear · 2 matte soft-touch · 1 liquid silicone · 1 leather · 1 with your artwork printed
- Pay for express shipping. Two weeks of waiting costs more than 30 KD of DHL.
- Have them print **one Arabic design** in the sample batch. How a factory handles Arabic
  artwork tells you everything about whether they'll ruin your best-selling family.

---

## Stage 4 · Quality testing — Weeks 6–7 (free, one evening)

Run this on every candidate and **film all of it** — this footage is your best pre-launch
content and your best "why we're different" proof.

| Test | Method | Pass criteria |
|---|---|---|
| Drop | 1.5 m and 2.0 m onto concrete and tile, 10 drops, all faces, with a real phone or a weighted dummy | No cracks, no corner separation, phone undamaged |
| Camera lip | Slide the phone face-down and lens-down across grit | Lens glass never contacts the surface |
| Button feel | 200 presses each | No stiffening, no rattle, defined click throughout |
| Fit | Install/remove 20 times | No stretching, no loose corners, cutouts perfectly aligned |
| MagSafe | Hang the phone from a MagSafe puck; car vent mount at speed | ≥ 1,100 gf hold, doesn't drop over a speed bump |
| Wireless charging | 15 W charger, 30 min | Charges, phone doesn't exceed normal warmth |
| Matte durability | Denim rub 100 strokes; then a car dashboard at Kuwaiti midday for 3 days | Still matte, no gloss patches, no warping |
| Yellowing (clear) | UV lamp 72 h, or dashboard for 2 weeks | No visible yellow shift |
| Oleophobic | Greasy fingerprints, wipe with a dry cloth | Cleans without a mark |
| Smell | Open the bag | No chemical/plastic smell. A smelly case is a lost customer |
| Print | Tape-pull test on printed artwork; alcohol wipe | No lift, no fade |

**Then live with the winner for a week.** Use it as your daily case. You'll find things no test
protocol catches.

## Stage 5 · Packaging production — Weeks 6–8 (~420–480 KD)
Per [`06-packaging-unboxing.md`](06-packaging-unboxing.md): boxes and mailers from China with
the case order; cards, stickers and colour-matched spine labels printed locally in Kuwait.
Order the paper items **before** the cases arrive so you can pack on day one.

## Stage 6 · Product photography — Weeks 8–9 (~250–500 KD)

**One day, two blocks, everything you need for three months.**

*Morning — studio/table (a rented studio hour, or a room with one window and a foam board):*
- Every SKU: front on-phone, back, 3/4, side profile
- Macro details: camera lip, buttons, MagSafe ring, deboss, foil
- Flat-lays: case + box, colourway family lined up, all 11 cases as a grid (your key
  collection image)
- A phone-on-tripod slow-mo of the magnetic box opening and the MagSafe snap

*Afternoon — golden hour on location (car interior, concrete stairwell, café table, corniche):*
- 3 friends/creators, styled, phone in hand — 60% of your final assets come from here
- Vertical video: walking with the phone, pulling it out of a pocket, snapping the wallet on,
  passing a case between two people

**Rules:** shoot **vertical first** (9:16), then crop to 4:5 and 1:1. Colour-grade everything
with **one** preset. Deliverables target: 60 stills + 20 vertical clips. Budget option: a
student photographer at 100–150 KD for the day plus a 150 KD studio hour; premium option a
freelance product photographer at 300–400 KD. Never, under any circumstances, use the
factory's photos.

## Stage 7 · Website — Weeks 7–11 (~250–400 KD year one)

Shopify Basic + a custom theme built from the `/site` prototype in this repo. Sequence:
1. Store skeleton, products, variants (model × colour), and the SKU scheme.
2. KNET + Apple Pay + Tabby live, and **test a real 1 KD order end to end** including refund.
3. All 11 pages populated with real copy (docs 1–6 have most of it written).
4. Arabic version, human-reviewed.
5. Shipping zones and rates: Kuwait (free over 15 KD, else 1.500), GCC, international.
6. Policies, order-confirmation emails, WhatsApp order updates, abandoned-cart flow.
7. Pixels, GA4, and a **launch-day soak test** — put 10 friends through checkout simultaneously.

**Have a fallback:** if the site slips, you can still launch on Instagram + WhatsApp with a
Google-Form-and-KNET-link flow. Cash flow beats elegance.

---

## Stage 8 · Instagram & TikTok setup — Week 6 (free)

Set both up **7 weeks before launch** and post from day one. A profile with 3 posts and no
history converts nobody.

- Business/creator accounts, identical handles, monogram profile picture, the bio template from
  doc 1, WhatsApp button connected.
- **First 9 posts, planned as a grid** so the profile looks intentional the moment anyone lands:
  brand statement · Arabic type detail · material macro · the tagline · a hand-held shot ·
  the box · the drop date · a founder note · the lookbook teaser.
- Highlights created empty and named: `DROP 01` · `CUSTOM` · `REVIEWS` · `SHIPPING` · `BTS`.
- **Post 5×/week on TikTok and 4×/week on Instagram from week 6.** Posting cadence before
  launch is the difference between 40 and 400 first-day visitors.

## Stage 9 · Pre-launch hype — Weeks 6–13 (~100–200 KD)

**The goal is not followers. It is a Drop Pass list of 300+ phone numbers before launch day.**
An owned list converts at 10–20×+ a cold feed.

**Week 6–8 — "something is being built"**
Faceless, textural, mysterious. Macro shots of samples with the design hidden. Films of the
drop tests. No brand reveal yet.

**Week 9–10 — the reveal**
Wordmark, story, and the Gold Route concept. First on-person imagery. Announce the drop date.
Open the **Drop Pass** signup: *"200 of each. Drop Pass members get in 30 minutes early."*

**Week 11–12 — proof and seeding**
Behind the scenes: packing, printing names, the box arriving. Creator seeding goes out.
Countdown begins. Publish the lookbook 48 h before launch to the Drop Pass only.

**Week 13 — 72-hour countdown**
Daily story countdowns, one case revealed per day, "set a reminder" stickers, a live pre-launch
Q&A in Stories.

### Named content ideas — TikTok
1. **"We drop-tested it from 2 metres so you don't have to."** Real concrete, real phone, one
   take, no music cut. Highest-trust video you can make.
2. **"POV: your case matches your outfit."** Four outfits, four cases, four hard cuts on the beat.
3. **"Writing your name in Arabic — properly."** Split screen: the broken Arabic everyone else
   prints vs. yours. This video will do numbers *and* teach the market why you're different.
4. **The UV printer ASMR series.** Machine printing a name, no talking, no music, satisfying.
   Once you own the printer this is infinite content.
5. **"Rating phone cases in Kuwait, including ours."** Honest, self-deprecating, ends on your
   own case. Fastest route to credibility with this audience.
6. **"How many cases does she own?"** Street/campus interviews at GUST, The Avenues, a café.
   You get UGC, reach, and market research in the same afternoon.
7. **"200 only. Here's why."** Founder to camera, 40 seconds, explaining the drop promise.
   Positions you as a brand with principles, not a shop with stock.
8. **Matchday build.** "Making a case for the derby in 60 seconds" — colourway + number.
9. **"Cheap case vs ALSAFRAN"** — side-by-side abuse test. Never name the competitor.
10. **The packing series.** "Packing order #001." Do this for the first 20 orders. People are
    fascinated by a business starting, and it makes early buyers feel historic.
11. **Arabic word of the week** — the meaning and the calligraphy behind each design.
12. **"Which one, 1 2 3 or 4?"** — the single highest-engagement format in existence, and it
    doubles as demand research for the next drop.

### Named content ideas — Instagram Reels
1. **The 20-second drop film** — Gold Route, all 11 cases, one continuous camera move, gold
   flare, no talking.
2. **Unboxing in one take** — the seven-beat choreography from doc 6, shot from above.
3. **"Snap."** Ultra-slow-motion MagSafe wallet snapping onto the case. Loop it seamlessly.
4. **Colour-match transitions** — hand covers lens, case + outfit both change. Four beats.
5. **"How it's made"** — factory line clips (ask the supplier for footage) → box → your hands →
   customer's hands.
6. **Customer repost Fridays** — a fixed weekly Reel of the week's UGC. Consistency creates a
   ritual and drives more UGC.
7. **The archive Reel** — after drop 01 sells out: "Gone. 200/200." Pure scarcity proof, and it
   sells drop 02 harder than any ad.

### Named content ideas — Stories
- **Daily countdown** with the number sticker, one case teased per day
- **Poll: "Qahwa or Bone?"** — engagement plus real range planning
- **"Ask us anything about the cases"** question box, answered on camera
- **Behind the scenes:** the boxes arriving, the first pack, the courier pickup
- **Live sell-through updates on launch day:** `61/200 gone in 40 minutes` — nothing drives
  urgency like watching stock disappear in real time
- **Repost every single tagged customer**, always, within an hour
- **"Swipe up: Drop Pass"** on repeat during the final week

### Campaign concepts
| Campaign | Idea |
|---|---|
| **THE GOLD ROUTE** (launch) | The saffron trade route story. Film: a case travelling — dhow, sea, sand, city, a hand. Ties the brand story to the product in 30 seconds |
| **WEAR YOUR PHONE** (always-on) | 20 people in Kuwait, each with the case that matches their fit. Rolling series, one per week, endless UGC |
| **NO. 041 / 200** | Portraits of real customers holding their numbered case with their number shown. Turns buyers into the campaign — and makes the numbering feel like membership |
| **MATCHDAY** (seasonal) | 48-hour capsule before a derby or the Gulf Cup. Countdown, colourway, name + number |
| **MY NAME IN ARABIC** | People seeing their own name properly set for the first time. Genuinely emotional, extremely shareable, and unownable by competitors |
| **BACK TO CAMPUS** (Sept) | Campus takeover: stands at GUST/AUK/KU, a student ambassador per campus, "new semester, new case" |
| **EIDIYA** (Eid) | Gift box + personalised name as the default Eid gift under 15 KD |

## Stage 10 · Kuwait micro-influencers — Weeks 10–12 (~150–350 KD in product)

**Go micro and go local.** Twenty creators at 5K–40K followers will out-perform one at 400K,
cost a tenth as much, and look far more credible.

**Who:** university students with real engagement, football content creators, car/streetwear
accounts, specialty-coffee and lifestyle accounts, local photographers, gaming creators, and
smaller fashion/OOTD accounts. **Check comment quality, not follower count** — 5% engagement
on 8K beats 0.4% on 200K.

**The offer, in order of preference:**
1. **Seeding (free product, no obligation)** — send 20–30 personalised cases in full packaging
   with a handwritten note using their name. No contract, no brief, no demand. A meaningful
   share will post because the box is worth posting, and unpaid posts read as genuine.
2. **Affiliate** — a personal code (`NOOR10`), 15% commission plus a 10% discount for their
   audience. Zero upfront cost, scales with performance, and doubles as your attribution data.
3. **Paid, only for proven performers** — 20–75 KD for a Reel/TikTok from creators whose seeded
   post actually converted. Never pay before you've seen someone sell.
4. **Ambassadors (month 3+)** — 3–5 creators, one per campus/subculture, on a small monthly
   product allowance plus commission, featured in your campaigns. Long-term association beats
   one-off posts.

**Make it easy to post:** send a personalised case in the full box, include a card with their
name written by hand, add a small card listing the handle, the hashtag and the drop date, and
follow up **once** with a thank-you and nothing else. Never ask for a post twice.

## Stage 11 · Launch day — Week 13

**Timing: Thursday, 8:00 PM Kuwait time.** Thursday evening is peak weekend-mode browsing;
8 PM beats the late-night scroll and leaves you a full weekend of momentum.

**The run of show**
| Time | Action |
|---|---|
| T−48 h | Lookbook to Drop Pass only. Final countdown story. |
| T−24 h | Founder video: "tomorrow, 8 PM, 200 of each." |
| T−3 h | Story countdown sticker. Creator posts go live. |
| **T−30 min** | **Drop Pass early access opens** (password page or a members-only link) |
| **T−0** | Site opens publicly. Post the drop Reel + TikTok simultaneously. WhatsApp broadcast. |
| T+30 min | First sell-through story: `X / 200 gone`. |
| T+2 h | Repost every tagged post. Answer every DM. Go live for 15 minutes. |
| T+24 h | "Day one" recap, thank-you post, and a real number ("184 cases, 11 hours"). |

**Be ready for:** everyone messaging at once (pre-write six DM replies), the wrong model
questions (a pinned "which iPhone do I have?" story), and a payment failure (have the KNET link
fallback ready). **Pack and ship the first orders within 24 hours** — early reviews decide
everything that follows.

**Also on launch day:** a physical moment. A small table at a café or on campus with the cases
out. Thirty people touching the product in person creates disproportionate word of mouth in a
market this size.

## Stage 12 · The first 100 orders — Weeks 13–20

The goal isn't revenue, it's **proof**: reviews, UGC, repeat rate and real demand data.

| Lever | Action |
|---|---|
| **Over-deliver on the first 30** | Free personalisation, an extra sticker sheet, a handwritten note. These 30 people become your marketing |
| **Ask for the review** | WhatsApp, 4 days after delivery, one line: *"Did it arrive okay? If you liked it, a photo review helps us a lot: [link]"*. Target 30+ photo reviews from the first 100 orders |
| **Repost everything** | Every tagged post, within the hour, always |
| **Ship fast** | Same-day dispatch inside Kuwait. Speed generates more word of mouth than any discount |
| **Read your data** | Which model, which colourway, which family, which price point. This decides drop 02 — not your taste |
| **Chase the sell-out** | If one design lags, don't discount it: bundle it, restyle it in new content, or feature it on a creator |
| **Publish the milestone** | "First 100 orders. Thank you." Momentum is content, and it makes the brand look established |
| **Open the pipeline** | Announce the drop 02 date the day drop 01 sells out. Never let the audience go quiet |

## Stage 13 · Customer retention

Acquisition costs money; retention prints it. Target **≥ 25% of customers buying again within
6 months**.

1. **The Drop Pass** is the retention product: 30-minute early access, drop lookbooks first,
   a birthday gift, and first refusal on collabs. Membership feels like status, not a mailing list.
2. **The accessory ladder** — a MagSafe wallet, strap or AirPods case offered 3 weeks after the
   first purchase, colour-matched to what they already bought. This is the single highest-
   converting message you will ever send.
3. **Six drops a year** — a built-in reason to come back roughly every two months.
4. **Case Trade** (month 6+): send back an old ALSAFRAN case, get 3 KD credit toward a new one.
   Refurbish or recycle the returns. Sustainability story, repeat purchase, and a competitor
   moat in one mechanic.
5. **WhatsApp, not email.** In this market a broadcast list is worth many times a newsletter.
   Keep it to 3–4 messages a month, all of them genuinely interesting.
6. **Service as marketing:** replace defects instantly, no argument, no proof-of-purchase
   theatre. One generous replacement produces a lifetime customer and a story they'll tell.
7. **The `No. ___ / 200` community** — a highlight, a repost ritual, and eventually a private
   group. People stay for belonging, not for cases.

## Stage 14 · GCC expansion — Month 4+
Sequence, requirements and per-market pricing are in
[`04-kuwait-gcc-market.md`](04-kuwait-gcc-market.md#44-gcc-expansion-sequence): Kuwait →
Saudi (the prize) → UAE (the credibility market) → Qatar & Bahrain → Oman → international.

**The gate before any new market:** Kuwait repeat rate above 20%, one drop fully sold out,
50+ photo reviews, and positive contribution per order. Expanding on top of an unproven funnel
just loses money faster in a second currency.
