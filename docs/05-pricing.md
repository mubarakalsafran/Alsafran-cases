# 5. Pricing Strategy (KWD)

> All figures in Kuwaiti Dinar (KD), 3 decimals, as used locally.
> FX assumption throughout: **1 KD ≈ 3.26 USD**.
> Factory costs are realistic FOB China estimates at **MOQ 300–500 units per design**, and
> should be treated as a planning model to validate against real quotes, not as fixed truth.

## 5.1 How a landed cost is actually built

Every "manufacturing cost" below is a **landed** cost — the number that matters. Quoting
yourself the factory's FOB price is the classic first-year mistake.

```
  FOB factory price
+ international freight (air 8–12 KD/kg; sea ~1/5 of that but 30–45 days)
+ Kuwait customs duty (~5% of CIF value)
+ clearing, handling, local transport
+ defect/reject allowance (3–5% of units)
= LANDED UNIT COST      ← plan with this
+ packaging
= COST OF GOODS SOLD (COGS)
```

Practical planning multiplier: **landed ≈ FOB × 1.30** by air on small consignments, dropping
toward **× 1.15** by sea at volume. Ship your first order by air (speed beats a few hundred
fils while you're learning); ship reorders by sea.

## 5.3 Bundle pricing
Bundles are how you raise average order value **without ever discounting a case's list price**.
The saving is framed as *"one box, one price"*, never as a percentage off.

| Bundle | Contents | Normal | Bundle price | Saving | COGS | GP | Margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The Swap Pack** | 2 × Signature (same phone, 2 looks) | 17.800 | **15.900** | 1.900 | 5.215 | 10.685 | **67.2%** |
| **The Gift Set** | Signature + name personalisation + gift box + note | 11.900+ | **14.900** | — | 3.900 | 11.000 | **73.8%** |
| **The Mag Set** | Signature case + MagSafe wallet + wrist strap, colour-matched | 29.700 | **24.900** | 4.800 | 7.100 | 17.800 | **71.5%** |
| **Drop Complete** | All 3 hero cases from a drop, in a collector box | 26.700 | **23.900** | 2.800 | 7.800 | 16.100 | **67.4%** |
| **Duo (friends)** | 2 × Core, any 2 phones | 11.800 | **9.900** | 1.900 | 2.900 | 7.000 | **70.7%** |
| **Team Pack** (Pitch) | 5 × Pitch with names & numbers | 64.500 | **54.900** | 9.600 | 17.350 | 37.550 | **68.4%** |

**Free-shipping threshold: 15.000 KD.** Set deliberately just above the Signature price so the
natural next step is a bundle. Delivery inside Kuwait costs you ~1.500–2.000 KD, so a 15 KD
basket at ~70% margin absorbs it comfortably.

## 5.4 The three positioning options

You must choose the brand's **centre of gravity**, because it dictates factory choice,
packaging spend, photography budget and who follows you. All three can work — they just can't
coexist.

### Option A — AFFORDABLE (mass youth)
| | |
|---|---|
| Price band | 3.900 – 6.900 KD |
| Hero price | **4.900 KD** |
| Landed + packaging | ~1.100 + 0.250 |
| GP per case | ~3.550 · **72.4%** |
| Volume needed to make 1,000 KD/month gross | **~280 cases** |
| Strategy | Win on volume and reach; thin packaging, broad model coverage, frequent restocks |
| Pros | Fast first sales, low inventory risk per SKU, big follower growth |
| Cons | Competes directly with every reseller in Kuwait; you will be undercut; **no route to a lifestyle brand**; personalisation and drops both feel wrong at this price |
| Verdict | ❌ This is a shop, not a brand. It forfeits the entire premise. |

### Option B — AFFORDABLE-PREMIUM (recommended) ⭐
| | |
|---|---|
| Price band | 5.900 – 13.500 KD, with limiteds at 16.900–19.900 |
| Hero price | **8.900 KD (Signature)** |
| GP per case | 4.330 – 9.000 · **67–73%** |
| Volume needed for 1,000 KD/month gross | **~166 cases** |
| Strategy | Core as the entry door, Signature as the hero, Atelier and Limiteds as the halo. Real packaging, real photography, drops, personalisation |
| Pros | 8.900 KD is still an impulse buy for the target audience but funds a premium experience; wide enough ladder to serve a 17-year-old and a 28-year-old; leaves room to raise prices later; supports the whole accessories roadmap |
| Cons | Requires genuine execution — the box, the type and the photography all have to be good, or the price looks unjustified |
| Verdict | ✅ **Recommended.** The only option that matches the brief: premium feel, youth reach, and international headroom. |

### Option C — PREMIUM (luxury accessories)
| | |
|---|---|
| Price band | 14.900 – 34.900 KD |
| Hero price | **19.900 KD** |
| Landed + packaging | ~4.500 + 1.400 |
| GP per case | ~14.000 · **70.4%** |
| Volume needed for 1,000 KD/month gross | **~72 cases** |
| Strategy | Leather, small numbered runs, boutique consignment, near-zero discounting |
| Pros | Beautiful margins per unit, low volume needed, strong PR angle |
| Cons | Cuts out segments A and C (students and football kids) — most of your brief's audience; needs 2–3× the launch budget for materials, packaging and photography; needs brand equity you don't have yet |
| Verdict | ⚠️ Not a launch position — but it's the right position for the **Atelier tier and collabs**, which is exactly how Option B already uses it. Revisit as a standalone line in Year 2–3. |

**Chosen strategy: Option B, with Option C's tier living inside it as the halo.** This is the
classic and correct fashion structure: an accessible entry price, a hero mid-tier that carries
the volume and the margin, and scarce top-end product that sets perception for everything below.

## 5.5 From gross margin to money in the bank
Gross margin is not profit. The real per-order picture at the recommended positioning:

| Line | KD | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Average order value | 12.500 | One Signature + some bundle/personalisation mix |
| Gross profit at ~70% | 8.750 | |
| Payment processing (KNET flat fee / card ~2.5%) | −0.300 | |
| Delivery cost net of what the customer pays | −0.700 | |
| Customer acquisition (blended: content-led + some paid) | −3.000 | Target ≤ 3.500 |
| **Contribution per order** | **≈ 4.750** | **≈ 38% of revenue** |

**Fixed monthly overhead (lean startup):** Shopify Basic ~12 · apps 15 · domain/email 3 ·
content production 100 · packaging top-ups & misc 40 ≈ **170 KD/month**.

**Break-even ≈ 36 orders/month** (~450 KD revenue). At 100 orders/month (~1,250 KD revenue)
contribution is ~475 KD, so roughly **300 KD/month of real profit** — which is the point at
which you reinvest in the UV printer.

### Pricing rules to hold the line
1. **Never discount a case's list price.** Add value instead: a free sticker sheet, a strap, a
   free personalisation, early drop access. One 30%-off weekend permanently resets what your
   customers believe the product is worth.
2. **The only permitted price mechanics:** bundles, a 15 KD free-shipping threshold, a first-
   order gift (not a discount), a student-verified 10% *at the till only* — never advertised in
   the feed, and a birthday gift for Drop Pass members.
3. **Limiteds never go on sale.** They sell out or they sit; either outcome protects the brand.
4. **Price in confident numbers.** 5.900 / 8.900 / 13.500 / 16.900. No 5.750, no 8.250, and
   never a `.99` — that reads discount, not design.
5. **Raise prices with the product, not with inflation.** When Signature moves to a better
   finish or a new magnet array, it becomes 9.900 KD and you say exactly why.
