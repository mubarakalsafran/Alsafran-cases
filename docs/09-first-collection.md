# 9. First Collection — DROP 01: **GOLD ROUTE**

## 9.1 The collection idea

> **GOLD ROUTE** — طريق الذهب
> *Eleven cases about the most precious thing that ever crossed this water.*

Saffron travelled from Persia and India, through the Gulf, on the same dhows that carried
Kuwait's pearls out. The route is the collection: the **sea** (Cobalt, Pearl), the **cargo**
(Safran Gold, Qahwa), the **coast** (Sand, Bone), and the **city it built** (Ink, `965`).

That's what makes these eleven read as a *collection* rather than eleven designs:

1. **One palette, no exceptions.** Every case is drawn from the Gold Route palette — Bone,
   Ink, Sand, Qahwa, Pearl, Cobalt, Safran Gold, Sadu Red. Nothing outside it ships.
2. **One shared detail.** Every case in the drop carries the **Saffron Thread** — a single
   hairline stroke with three strands — somewhere: foiled, debossed, or printed at 8 mm. Line
   the eleven up and the same mark appears on all of them.
3. **One naming logic.** Single words, uppercase, drawn from the route. `BONE` `INK` `CRYSTAL`
   `SAFRAN` `QAHWA` `YALLA` `SADU` `PITCH` `LULU` `DHOW` `965`.
4. **One typographic system.** Latin in the brand grotesque, Arabic in geometric Kufi, numerals
   in the athletic condensed. No fifth font anywhere.
5. **One grade.** Every image in the lookbook is shot at golden hour or in low key with one gold
   rim light, and graded with one LUT.
6. **A deliberate range shape.** A gateway (Crystal), a hero (Bone/Ink/Qahwa), a
   conversation-starter (Yalla, Sadu), a culture play (Pitch), a halo (Lulu, Dhow) and a
   collectible (965). Every price point and every one of the four audience segments is covered
   exactly once.

## 9.5 The lookbook
Shoot the collection as one story, not eleven product listings:

1. **The group shot** — all eleven, lined up on sand-coloured plaster, hard 4 PM shadow. This
   is the image that proves it's a collection. It goes everywhere.
2. **Four pairs** — Bone & Ink, Crystal & Safran, Qahwa & Sadu, Pitch & 965. Pairs sell the
   Swap Pack bundle.
3. **Six on-person portraits** — one per segment, styled, shot on location (car interior,
   campus stairwell, café table, football bench, corniche, majlis).
4. **The route film** (30 s vertical) — sea → sand → gold thread → hand → case. The brand story
   in one scroll.
5. **Eleven macro details** — one per case, all lit identically, posted as a grid across the
   drop week. Eleven posts, one visual language, zero extra shoot cost.
