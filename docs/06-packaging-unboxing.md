# 6. Packaging & Unboxing

## 6.1 The strategic point

Packaging is the only part of the brand the customer physically holds before they see the
product. It is also the cheapest advertising you will ever buy: a box that's worth filming
turns every customer into a content producer. Budget for it as **marketing**, not overhead.

Design constraint we're working to: **premium enough to film, cheap enough to send 1,000 of
them.** Everything below lands between **0.400 and 0.900 KD per order** — 5–7% of an average
order — which is the right number for this positioning.

**Startup ordering note:** rigid boxes are the one item with a real MOQ (usually 500–1,000).
Order **one Ink box with a blank spine** and print **colour-matched spine labels locally** in
small runs — that gives you per-colourway boxes without per-colourway box MOQs. This single
trick saves ~800 KD on the first order.

## 6.4 The unboxing choreography

Design the *sequence*, not just the pieces. Seven beats, roughly 40 seconds — the exact length
of a TikTok.

1. **Weight.** The mailer feels heavier than expected. (The rigid box does this for free; it's
   the first premium cue and it happens before anything is opened.)
2. **Resistance.** The gold seal has to be broken. A tiny ceremonial act — and a great
   close-up shot.
3. **Reveal 1.** The Ink box, gold foil catching the light. *Slow* magnetic flap — magnetic
   closures are the single most-filmed packaging feature in existence.
4. **A message.** Inside the lid, the Saffron Thread and one line: *Wear your phone. البسه، لا
   تخبيه.* Nothing else. The reader has a moment of "these people thought about this".
5. **The product.** Tissue leaf lifted, case seated in its Bone recess, colour popping against
   the off-white.
6. **The extras.** Under the tray: the card with their name written by hand, the sticker sheet,
   the cloth. Bonus items always come *after* the product, never before.
7. **The invitation.** The card's back asks for exactly one thing, and it's easy:
   > **You're No. 041 of 200.**
   > Post it. Tag **@alsafran.co**. We repost the best one every Friday — and reposted
   > customers get first access to the next drop.
   > `[QR]` → the DROP 01 film & your care guide

**Why the handwritten name matters:** it takes four seconds and it is the highest-ROI action in
the entire operation. It is the thing customers photograph and mention, and it is literally
impossible for a dropshipper to fake at scale.

## 6.6 The QR code — one code, many jobs
Point the QR at a **single dynamic short link** you control (e.g. `alsafran.co/d1`) so you can
change its destination after the boxes are printed. Land it on a mobile page with four things:

1. **The drop film** (30–60 s vertical) — the lookbook, in motion.
2. **Care guide** — how to keep matte matte, how to clean a clear case. Reduces "it yellowed"
   complaints.
3. **Drop Pass signup** — one tap to join the WhatsApp broadcast list. *This is the real
   business purpose of the QR: converting a one-time buyer into an owned audience member.*
4. **"Post your unboxing"** — a repost gallery plus a 100 KD-value monthly prize for the best
   one.

Track scans. Scan rate is a direct measure of how good your packaging is.

## 6.7 The social/UGC message

**In the box (short, specific, one ask):**
> Post it. Tag **@alsafran.co**. Best one every Friday gets reposted — and reposted customers
> get first access to the next drop.

**The mechanic that actually generates UGC** — early access is worth more than a discount to
this audience:

| Trigger | Reward |
|---|---|
| Tag us in a post or story | Reposted + entered into the monthly prize |
| Post an unboxing video (any length) | **Free personalisation on your next order** |
| Reposted by us | **Drop Pass status** — 30 min early access to every future drop |
| Bring a friend (their code) | Both get a free sticker pack + strap on the next order |

**Sticker sheet as a growth device:** stickers go on laptops, water bottles, phone backs, car
windows and gym bags. A 0.055 KD sticker seen by 200 people on a laptop in a GUST library is
the cheapest media buy available to you. Always include more stickers than the customer needs
— they'll give the extras away, which is the point.

## 6.8 Cost-efficiency rules for a startup
1. **One box, many spines.** As above — blank Ink box, locally printed colour-matched labels.
2. **Ink + Bone + gold foil only.** A two-colour palette plus one foil is dramatically cheaper
   than full-colour CMYK print and looks more expensive. Restraint is free luxury.
3. **Moulded pulp beats plastic** on cost, on feel and on the sustainability story.
4. **Buy paper items locally in Kuwait** (cards, stickers, spine labels): no MOQ pain, no
   freight, 3-day turnaround, and you can iterate the copy every drop.
5. **Buy boxes and mailers from China** with the case order — they ship in the same consignment,
   so freight is nearly free.
6. **Skip the ribbon, the void filler and the branded tape at launch.** Nobody films those.
7. **Spend the saved money on foil and the magnetic closure.** Those are the two things people
   actually notice on camera.
8. **First order quantities:** 500 Signature boxes, 300 Core boxes, 1,000 cards, 1,000 sticker
   sheets, 1,000 mailers, 500 seals. Roughly **420–480 KD** all-in, and it covers your first
   ~800 orders.
