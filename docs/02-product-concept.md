# 2. Product Concept

## 2.1 Product architecture — three tiers, eight style families

Everything ALSAFRAN makes fits one of three **tiers** (which set price and material quality)
and one of eight **style families** (which set the design language). Tier answers *"how much
and how good?"*; family answers *"who is this for?"*. Keeping these two axes separate is what
lets you scale to 60 SKUs without the range turning into a jumble.

```
                         TIER (price & build)
                CORE            SIGNATURE          ATELIER
              5.900 KD          8.900 KD          13.500 KD
            ┌──────────────┬──────────────────┬──────────────────┐
  Minimal   │      ●       │        ●         │        ●         │
  Clear     │      ●       │        ●         │                  │
  Matte     │      ●       │        ●         │        ●         │
  Sport     │              │        ●         │        ●         │
  Street    │      ●       │        ●         │                  │
  Arabic    │              │        ●         │        ●         │
  Limited   │              │                  │        ● 200 pcs │
  Custom    │              │        ●         │        ●         │
            └──────────────┴──────────────────┴──────────────────┘
```

### The three tiers

| | **CORE** | **SIGNATURE** | **ATELIER** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | 5.900 KD | 8.900 KD | 13.500 KD |
| Build | Hybrid PC shell + TPU bumper | Soft-touch PC + reinforced TPU, anodised alloy buttons | Liquid silicone, Alcantara, or full-grain / Saffiano leather over a PC frame |
| MagSafe | On clear SKUs only | **Every SKU** — 12× N42 ring | **Every SKU** — 16× N52 ring + alignment magnet |
| Drop rating | 1.5 m | 2.0 m (MIL-STD-810G style test) | 2.0 m |
| Finish life | Standard | Anti-yellow, oleophobic top coat | Anti-yellow, anti-microbial, edge-sealed |
| Purpose | Volume, entry price, gifting, first purchase | **The hero tier — 60% of revenue** | Halo, limited drops, collabs, press |
| Personalisation | No | Yes (+3.000 KD) | Yes (+3.000 KD, foil or deboss) |

### The eight style families

**1 · MINIMAL — "the everyday"**
Single-colour, no logo on the face, monogram debossed *inside* the bottom edge where only the
owner sees it. Bone, Ink, Sand, Pearl. The case for someone who doesn't want a case to be
noticed but wants it to feel right. Highest repeat-purchase rate; this is the family that
builds your reorder business.

**2 · CLEAR — "the honest one"**
Anti-yellowing hybrid: German Bayer/Covestro-grade transparent PC back + TPU frame, edge
bonded so no line shows. The **gold MagSafe ring is visible through the back** — that ring is
the brand's signature detail and instantly identifies an ALSAFRAN case at two metres. Optional
soft frosted-Bone frame version.

**3 · MATTE — "the one people touch"**
Soft-touch / velvet-coat PC in the seasonal colourways. This is where premium is *felt*: a
proper matte coat resists fingerprints, doesn't turn glossy on the corners after two months in
a Kuwaiti summer, and has a faint grip. Test candidates hard — bad matte is the number one
quality failure in this category.

**4 · SPORT / PITCH — "matchday"**
Football-culture DNA without licensed logos: kit-inspired colour blocking, sleeve stripes,
jersey-mesh texture panels, a large condensed number on the back, contrast-taped edges.
Personalisable with **name + number**. Colourways timed to the Kuwaiti league, Gulf Cup, El
Clásico and the World Cup cycle. Also covers gym/running: sweat-resistant grip texture and a
version with a lanyard anchor.

**5 · STREET — "the loud one"**
Graphic-led: bold type lockups, all-over monogram (SFRN repeat), spray/stencil textures,
Arabic + Latin bilingual graphics, gradient dip-dye. Small collabs with Kuwaiti illustrators
and 3D artists. This is the family that goes viral; keep it 20% of the range and never let it
define the brand.

**6 · ARABIC TYPOGRAPHY — "the one that travels"**
The most defensible family you own. Words chosen for how they *look* and how they land:
`يلا` (yalla) · `الحمدلله` · `قهوة` · `حبيبي` · `صفران` · `965` · `توكلت` · `أنا كويتي`.
Rendered in three treatments — modern geometric Kufi, a soft contemporary Naskh, and a
stencil/athletic Arabic — each drawn or licensed, kerned by hand, reviewed by a native reader.
Available in deboss, gold foil, tonal (same-colour raised) and full-colour print.
**Rule: every Arabic design is signed off by a native speaker before tooling. No exceptions.**

**7 · LIMITED / DROP — "the archive"**
200 units, one production run, never repeated. Individually numbered `No. 041 / 200` on the
inside of the case *and* on the box. Ships with a card carrying the number and drop name.
Retail 14.900–19.900 KD. Two to four per year, plus collabs. These generate the story, the
scarcity and the resale conversations that make the whole brand look bigger than it is.

**8 · CUSTOM / PERSONALISED — "yours"**
Name, initials, number, date, coordinates, or a short phrase, in Arabic or Latin, positioned
by the customer in a live web preview and produced in-house in Kuwait on a UV printer or laser.
Three placements (bottom-centre, back-centre, vertical-left), four finishes (white, black,
gold, deboss), five curated type styles. 3–4 working days. This is your margin engine and your
gift business.

## 2.2 Materials & construction spec

### Shell system
- **CORE:** 1.2 mm polycarbonate back + 1.8 mm TPU perimeter, twin-shot (co-moulded) so there
  is no glue line and no separation after a season.
- **SIGNATURE:** 1.4 mm soft-touch-coated PC back + reinforced TPU frame with **internal
  honeycomb / air-cushion corners**. Anodised 6063 aluminium buttons.
- **ATELIER:** PC frame + wrapped exterior — liquid silicone (with a **microfibre lining**,
  which is what stops the "cheap silicone" feel), Alcantara, or full-grain/Saffiano leather
  with edge paint and stitched detail.

### Finishes to offer (and how to talk about them)
| Finish | Feel | Sell it as |
|---|---|---|
| Soft-touch matte | Velvet, warm, grippy | "Doesn't go shiny. Doesn't take fingerprints." |
| Anti-yellow clear | Glassy, cool | "Still clear in a year — Bayer-grade PC, not resin." |
| Liquid silicone + microfibre | Skin-like, dense | "Lined, so it doesn't squeak or stretch." |
| Leather / Saffiano | Structured, ages | "Gets better. Edge-painted, not raw-cut." |
| Tonal deboss | Invisible until light hits | "You'll know it's there. Nobody else will." |
| 24k-look gold foil | Sharp, precious | "Real foil, hot-stamped. Not printed gold ink." |

### Protection specification (state these numbers publicly — they sell)
- **Raised camera lip: 1.4 mm** above the tallest lens, with a chamfered inner wall so it
  doesn't chip. Optional **integrated lens ring guard** in anodised alloy on Signature+.
- **Raised screen lip: 1.1 mm** — enough that a face-down phone rests on the case, compatible
  with edge-to-edge screen protectors.
- **Corner impact structure:** four internal air-cushion pockets; 2.0 m drop rating on
  Signature and Atelier, 1.5 m on Core.
- **Buttons:** anodised aluminium (Signature/Atelier) or reinforced TPU (Core) with a defined
  4-way click. **Button feel is the single most-returned complaint in this category** — every
  sample gets 200 press cycles before approval.
- **Ports:** oversized, chamfered charging cutout that fits third-party braided cables and a
  Qi/MagSafe puck simultaneously.
- **Grip:** micro-textured side rails (not smooth) so the phone doesn't slide off a car
  dashboard.
- **Wireless:** all MagSafe SKUs tested with 15 W Apple charger, a car vent mount, and a
  PopSocket-style ring to confirm hold strength (target ≥ 1,100 gf).

### MagSafe policy
- **Signature and Atelier: MagSafe on every single SKU.** No "MagSafe version" upsell. Once a
  customer owns MagSafe accessories they cannot buy a non-MagSafe case again, and this is the
  hook into your entire accessories roadmap (wallets, straps, mounts, power banks).
- **Core: MagSafe only on Clear.** This is the deliberate gap that upsells to Signature.
- Ring construction: sealed magnet array **inside** the shell (never a visible glued-in
  circle), 12× N42 on Signature, 16× N52 + alignment magnet on Atelier.
- Marketing name: call it **"Mag"** on non-Apple models later (`ALSAFRAN Mag`) to stay clear of
  Apple's trademark rules on Samsung/Android SKUs.

## 2.3 Model coverage & scaling plan

Do **not** launch wide. The single biggest cash killer in this business is dead stock on models
nobody in your market carries.

**Launch (Phase 1) — 4 models only:**
1. iPhone 17 Pro Max
2. iPhone 17 Pro
3. iPhone 16 Pro Max
4. iPhone 16 Pro

**Phase 2 (weeks 6–10, after real demand data) — add:**
5. iPhone 17 · 6. iPhone 16 · 7. iPhone 15 Pro Max · 8. iPhone 15 Pro

**Phase 3 (month 4–6):** iPhone 14 / 14 Pro, iPhone 13, plus **Samsung S25 Ultra and S25** —
Samsung Ultra buyers in the Gulf are a genuinely underserved premium segment and almost no
design-led brand serves them.

**Phase 4 (month 9+):** Samsung S24 Ultra, Z Fold/Flip (very high margin, near-zero
competition on design), Google Pixel Pro if data supports it.

**The system that makes this scalable:**
- **Design once, apply to a mould library.** Every graphic is authored on a master artboard
  with a per-model die-line overlay. Adding a model = new blank + re-export, not a redesign.
- **Model-agnostic naming.** SKU format `ALS-D01-QAHWA-17PM-MAG`
  (`brand-drop-design-model-feature`). Never bake a model name into a product's public name.
- **Blanks + in-house printing for the long tail.** Once you own a UV printer, older and
  Android models are produced **on demand** from stocked blanks — zero dead stock, 2-day
  turnaround, and you can honestly say "we cover 40+ models" while only pre-stocking 8.
- **Depth over breadth on launch:** 100–150 units per design on the top two models, 40–60 on
  the others. Selling out is a marketing asset; dead stock is a cash coffin.

## 2.4 Packaging & premium detail cues
Full packaging system, costs and unboxing choreography are in
[`06-packaging-unboxing.md`](06-packaging-unboxing.md). In summary: a rigid magnetic-close
Ink box with a Bone interior and gold-foil monogram, a Saffron Thread print inside the lid,
a numbered card on limited drops, a sticker sheet, a QR to the drop film, and a wax-look
Gold seal sticker on the mailer.

### The eleven details that make a case feel expensive
These cost almost nothing and are what separates you from a dropshipper:
1. A **debossed monogram inside the bottom edge** — a private mark only the owner finds.
2. **Anodised metal buttons** with a crisp, defined click.
3. **Edge-sealed clear cases** with no visible seam or glue line.
4. A **1.4 mm chamfered** camera lip, not a blunt wall.
5. **Micro-textured rails** — grip you can feel but not see.
6. A **gold MagSafe ring** visible through clear backs (signature recognition cue).
7. **Microfibre lining** in every silicone and leather SKU.
8. A **matte coat that survives** the summer without polishing at the corners.
9. **Numbered** limited units, inside the case *and* on the box.
10. **Colour-matched packaging** — box print matches the case colourway, so the box is part of
    the product photo.
11. **A dust cloth** in Signature and Atelier boxes — costs fils, reads as luxury.
